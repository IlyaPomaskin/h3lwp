Animated live wallpaper that shows maps from Heroes of Might and Magic III.

*** REQUIRES YOUR OWN COPY OF THE GAME ***
No game content is included. You need Heroes 3: Shadow of Death or Complete Edition:
• the GOG offline installer (.exe + .bin), or
• the H3sprite.lod file from the game's Data folder.
HD Edition, Restoration of Erathia and Armageddon's Blade are not supported.

Horn of the Abyss (optional): select the official HotA installer from h3hota.com.

FEATURES
• Original and HotA maps, or your own .h3m maps
• Map change interval, scale, brightness, scrolling
• No internet permission, no ads, no tracking

FAQ
Q: Why can't I set it on the lock screen?
A: Some phones (often MIUI and Samsung) don't allow live wallpapers there.

Q: Will it drain my battery?
A: No. It uses less than 1% of battery per day, because the wallpaper works only while it is visible.

Q: Source code?
A: https://github.com/IlyaPomaskin/h3lwp (GPL-3.0)
